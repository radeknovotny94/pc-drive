Privacy policy – PC Drive

Last updated: 2026-11-12

  • PC Drive is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account.
  • Data from Google APIs (Drive files and metadata) is transferred only between Google and the owner's own computer. It is never sent to, stored on, or shared with any third-party server.
  • Access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or data sale of any kind.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
